## Goal

Introduce braiding via $R$-matrices, state the hexagon axiom, define topological spin $\theta_a$.

## Content

1. **Braiding.** Exchanging two anyons of types $a, b$ fusing to $c$ gives a phase $R^{ab}_c \in U(1)$ (abelian case) or a matrix (non-abelian).
2. **Hexagon axiom.** Consistency of $R$ with fusion via $F$ produces the hexagon equations. State; do not derive.
3. **Topological spin.** $\theta_a = R^{aa}_{(ab)} / \ldots$ for self-braiding. Physical interpretation: the phase picked up when one anyon rotates $2\pi$ around itself.
4. **Examples.** Toric code: all $\theta = 1$ except $\theta_\epsilon = -1$. Ising: $\theta_\sigma = e^{i\pi/8}$.
