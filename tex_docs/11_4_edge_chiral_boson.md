## Goal

Derive the edge theory of a Laughlin $\nu = 1/m$ droplet as a single chiral boson with specific compactification radius, and state the bulk-edge inflow: the bulk $U(1)_m$ CS action on a manifold with boundary picks up a shift exactly canceling the edge anomaly.

## Content

1. **Edge chiral boson.** Parametrize the boundary by a scalar $\phi$, with action $S_{\text{edge}} = (m/4\pi)\int dt dx (\partial_t \phi \partial_x \phi - v (\partial_x \phi)^2)$.
2. **Compactification**: $\phi \sim \phi + 2\pi$.
3. **Anomaly.** The edge chiral boson has a $U(1)$ anomaly that alone would be inconsistent on the 2D edge.
4. **Inflow.** The bulk $U(1)_m$ CS term on a manifold with boundary shifts under a background gauge transformation by exactly the edge anomaly, canceling it. This is the abelian specialization of the Callan–Harvey mechanism, and it's the same story as Chapter 8 §8.6–8.7.

## Acceptance criteria

- Inflow explained via cross-reference to Chapter 8.
