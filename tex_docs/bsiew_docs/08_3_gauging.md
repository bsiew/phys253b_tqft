## Goal

Define what it means to gauge a $q$-form symmetry, and work out the $\mathbb{Z}_N$ 1-form example explicitly (gauging the center symmetry of $SU(N)$ Yang–Mills, relating $SU(N)$ and $PSU(N)$ theories).

## Content

1. **Gauging recipe.** Sum over configurations of the $(q+1)$-form background field $B$ with appropriate weight.
2. **Worked example: gauge electric 1-form $\mathbb{Z}_N$ in 4D Maxwell.**
   - Start with free Maxwell theory with compact $U(1)$.
   - The 1-form electric symmetry is $U(1)^{(1)}_{\text{elec}}$; acts on Wilson lines.
   - Gauge a $\mathbb{Z}_N$ subgroup: sum over 2-form backgrounds $B$ with quantized fluxes mod $N$.
   - Result: restricts the allowed electric line charges to multiples of $N$, i.e. replaces $U(1)$ with $U(1)/\mathbb{Z}_N$.
3. **Consequence.** Gauging the 1-form symmetry produces magnetic monopoles as charged 0-form operators (of the dual symmetry).

## Acceptance criteria

- The Maxwell / $\mathbb{Z}_N$ example worked through in full.
- Forward-pointer to how this relates to Dijkgraaf–Witten.
