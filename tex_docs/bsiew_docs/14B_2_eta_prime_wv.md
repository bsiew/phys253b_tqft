## Goal

State and sketch the Witten–Veneziano relation connecting the $\eta'$ mass to the pure-Yang-Mills topological susceptibility, and cite lattice and holographic evidence.

## Content

1. **The formula.** $m_{\eta'}^2 + m_\eta^2 - 2m_K^2 = (2N_f / F_\pi^2) \chi_t^{YM}$. The left-hand side is measured hadron masses; the right-hand side is a pure-gauge topological observable.
2. **Interpretation.** $\chi_t^{YM}$ is the topological susceptibility of pure Yang-Mills (no quarks). It is the "missing mass" that the anomaly contributes. Hadron spectroscopy indirectly measures a topological quantity.
3. **Sketch of the argument.** Large-$N_c$ limit; $\eta'$ mass is $O(1/N_c)$; the anomaly contribution is proportional to $\chi_t^{YM} / F_\pi^2$.
4. **Lattice support.** Cichy et al. 2015: non-perturbative lattice test with $N_f = 2+1+1$ dynamical fermions. Result consistent with WV within errors.
5. **Holographic support.** Arean et al. 2021: holographic QCD computation of $\chi_t$. Independent cross-check from the AdS/CFT side.
