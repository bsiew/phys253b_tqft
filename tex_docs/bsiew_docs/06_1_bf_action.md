## Goal

Introduce BF theory in 3 dimensions: the action $S_{BF} = \frac{k}{2\pi}\int_M B \wedge F$ with $B$ a $(d-2)$-form Lagrange multiplier and $F = dA$ (or $F = dA + A\wedge A$ in the non-abelian case), derive the equations of motion $F = 0$ and $dB = 0$, and identify the natural observables.

## Content outline

1. Action. In 3D abelian: $S = \frac{k}{2\pi}\int_M B \wedge dA$ with $B$ a one-form. $k \in \mathbb{Z}$ for compact $U(1)$.
2. Equations of motion:
   - Vary $B$: $dA = 0$ (flatness of $A$).
   - Vary $A$: $dB = 0$ (flatness of $B$).
3. Observables:
   - Wilson line $W_q(C) = \exp(iq \oint_C A)$.
   - 't Hooft surface $T_n(\Sigma) = \exp(in \int_\Sigma B)$ for oriented surface $\Sigma$.
4. Gauge invariance: discuss compact vs non-compact case.

## Derivation details

- Do not just state $F = 0$; vary the action explicitly.
- Show the gauge transformations $A \to A + d\lambda$, $B \to B + d\chi$ and verify action invariance.

## Acceptance criteria

- All EOM derived, not stated.
- Both types of observables defined.
- Gauge invariance verified.
- ~2 pages.
