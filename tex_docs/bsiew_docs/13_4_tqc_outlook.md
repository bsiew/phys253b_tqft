## Goal

Connect the anyon formalism of Ch 10 to the question of universal quantum computation: which anyon systems would give universal TQC from braiding alone, and which require supplementary resources?

## Content

1. **Ising anyons.** Braiding generates the Clifford group only. Universal QC requires magic-state distillation (non-topological supplement). This is the nu=5/2 / Majorana platform.
2. **Fibonacci anyons.** Braiding alone is universal. $\sigma \times \sigma = 1 + \sigma$. No known natural realization. The theoretical gold standard.
3. **What experimental requirements are.** Gap > temperature, coherence time > braiding time, scalable quasiparticle creation and measurement. Current platforms (GaAs, graphene) satisfy some but not all.

## Acceptance criteria

- Ising vs Fibonacci contrast stated.
- Experimental requirements listed concretely.
