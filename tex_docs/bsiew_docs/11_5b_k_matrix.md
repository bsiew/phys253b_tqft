## Goal

Generalize the single-field Laughlin action to the multi-component $K$-matrix framework, derive Hall conductance, quasiparticle charges, and mutual statistics from $K^{-1}$, and briefly introduce the nonabelian Moore–Read state at $\nu = 5/2$.

## Content

1. **$K$-matrix action.** $N$ emergent $U(1)$ fields, charge vector $\mathbf{t}$, integer symmetric $K$.
2. **Observables from $K^{-1}$.** $\sigma_{xy}$, quasiparticle charges, mutual statistics, GSD.
3. **Worked example.** Halperin $(3,3,1)$: $\sigma_{xy} = 2e^2/5h$, charge $e/4$, angle $\pi/4$.
4. **Nonabelian states.** Moore–Read Pfaffian at $\nu = 5/2$ as $SU(2)_2$ CS theory. Fusion rule $\sigma \times \sigma = 1 + \psi$. Forward-pointer to Ch 10 comparison table and Ch 13 (TQC).
