## Goal

Connect the topological theta parameter to the strong CP problem, the experimental neutron EDM bound, and the axion solution. The axion mass is controlled by $\chi_t$, so this is the third observable where QCD topology becomes measurable.

## Content

1. **Strong CP problem.** Physical angle $\bar\theta = \theta + \arg\det M_q$. CP violation in strong interactions proportional to $\bar\theta$. The neutron EDM is the cleanest observable.
2. **Neutron EDM bound.** Abel et al. 2020: $|d_n| < 1.8 \times 10^{-26}\, e\,\text{cm}$ at 90% CL. This constrains $|\bar\theta| \lesssim 10^{-10}$. Why is $\bar\theta$ so small? This is the strong CP problem.
3. **Peccei-Quinn mechanism.** Promote $\bar\theta$ to a dynamical field $a(x)/f_a$ (the axion). The anomalous coupling $\mathcal{L} \supset (a/f_a) q(x)$ generates a potential $V(a) = \chi_t (1 - \cos(a/f_a))$. The axion relaxes to $\langle a \rangle = 0$, dynamically enforcing CP conservation.
4. **Axion mass.** $m_a^2 f_a^2 = \chi_t$. The axion mass is controlled by the same topological susceptibility from §14.1--14.2. $m_a \approx 6\,\mu\text{eV} \cdot (10^{12}\,\text{GeV} / f_a)$.
5. **Phenomenology.** Mass-coupling plane. Four experimental classes: haloscopes (ADMX), helioscopes (CAST/IAXO), spin precession (CASPEr), dielectric haloscopes (MADMAX). One paragraph each. KSVZ vs DFSZ model bands.
