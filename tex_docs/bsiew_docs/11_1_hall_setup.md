## Goal

Introduce the experimental setup: 2D electron gas (MOSFET or GaAs/AlGaAs heterostructure), strong perpendicular magnetic field, Landau levels, filling fraction $\nu = N_{\text{electrons}}/N_{\text{flux quanta}}$.

## Content

1. **Hall bar geometry** and transport experiment: measure $R_{xx}$ and $R_{xy}$.
2. **Landau levels**: degenerate 2D levels at $E_n = \hbar\omega_c(n + 1/2)$.
3. **Integer quantum Hall**: at integer $\nu$, $R_{xy} = h/(\nu e^2)$.
4. **Fractional quantum Hall**: at $\nu = 1/3, 1/5, 2/5, \ldots$ — plateaus appear. These are the topologically ordered phases.
