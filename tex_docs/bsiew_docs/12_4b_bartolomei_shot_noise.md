## Goal

Expand the parenthetical shot-noise citations from Ch 11 into a proper experimental subsection. Explain how noise measurements complement interferometry: interferometry measures braiding phases; shot-noise measures fractional charges.

## Content

1. **Bartolomei 2020.** Collider-style noise measurement at $\nu = 1/3$. What is the observable? Excess noise at a QPC when two dilute beams of quasiparticles collide. The noise signature depends on the statistics of the particles: for anyons with angle $\theta = \pi/3$, the auto- and cross-correlations differ from both bosonic and fermionic predictions.
2. **Earlier shot-noise.** Saminadayar 1997 and de-Picciotto 1997: tunneling current fluctuations at a QPC, measuring charge $e/3$ via the Fano factor. This is fractional charge, not fractional statistics.
3. **Complementarity.** Shot-noise measures charge ($Q = e/m$); interferometry measures braiding phase ($e^{2\pi i/m}$). Together they pin down the same effective theory from two independent directions.
