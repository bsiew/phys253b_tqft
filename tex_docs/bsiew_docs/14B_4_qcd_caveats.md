## Goal

Dedicated "iffiness" subsection for the 3+1d story, paralleling Ch 11 §11.7 for the 2+1d story. Identify which observables are topologically robust and which require model-dependent input.

## Content

1. **$F \wedge \tilde F$ is locally a total derivative, but not globally trivial.** The topological content is in the winding, not in the local expression.
2. **Instanton-gas reasoning is semiclassical.** At strong coupling (physical QCD), the semiclassical instanton gas is not a controlled approximation. The topological susceptibility $\chi_t$ is defined non-perturbatively, but the instanton-gas estimate of it is qualitative.
3. **$\chi_t$ is Euclidean and theory-side.** It enters hadronic observables indirectly: via the WV formula (§14.2) and the axion mass (§14.3). Lattice QCD and holographic QCD provide the bridge.
4. **Neutron EDM is clean; $\bar\theta$ extraction is not.** The measurement of $d_n$ is a precision low-energy experiment. Extracting $\bar\theta$ from it requires hadronic matrix elements (QCD sum rules or lattice), which introduce model dependence.
5. **Axion mass vs couplings.** $m_a \propto \sqrt{\chi_t} / f_a$ is topologically robust (comes directly from $\chi_t$). But photon, nucleon, and spin couplings depend on the UV completion (KSVZ vs DFSZ vs other models).
