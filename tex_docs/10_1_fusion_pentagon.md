## Goal

Introduce anyon fusion at review level: labels, fusion rules $a \times b = \sum_c N^c_{ab} c$, and state (but do not derive) the pentagon axiom.

## Content

1. **Labels.** A finite set of anyon types $\{1, a, b, \ldots\}$ with vacuum $1$.
2. **Abelian anyons.** $a \times b$ is a single label, no multiplicity. Example: $\mathbb{Z}_k$ anyons of Laughlin.
3. **Non-abelian anyons.** $a \times b = \sum_c N^c_{ab} c$ with multiplicities. Example: Ising anyons with $\sigma \times \sigma = 1 + \psi$.
4. **Pentagon axiom.** Associativity of fusion is encoded in $F$-symbols; the pentagon equation constrains them. **State; do not derive.**

## Acceptance criteria

- 1.5 pages.
- Three anyon examples: $\mathbb{Z}_k$, Ising, toric code.
- Pentagon stated with one-line explanation.
