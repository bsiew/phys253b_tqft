Chapter 1 welcomes the reader, states the scope, identifies the audience, and explains how to read the paper.

## Content

1. **§1.1 Scope and thesis.** Extended version of the abstract.
2. **§1.2 Target audience and prerequisites.** Grad student with Schwartz through Ch 30. Minor algebraic-topology exposure helpful but not required (Chapter 2 builds the machinery).
3. **§1.3 How to read the paper.**

   - Part II (Chs 9–13): physical applications. Can be read after any deep-dive Part-I chapter.
