## Goal

Identify Laughlin quasiparticles as endpoints of Wilson lines of the emergent gauge field $a$, derive their charge $e/m$ and braiding phase $2\pi/m$, and cite the CS chapter's abelian Wilson-loop result (Example `ex:abelian-wilson`).

## Content

1. **Quasiparticle as Wilson-line endpoint.** Insert a Wilson line $W(C) = \exp(iq \oint_C a)$ for some charge $q$; an endpoint is a point-like excitation with $a$-flux attached.
2. **Charge.** Coupling $(1/2\pi) a \wedge dA_{\text{ext}}$ forces the quasiparticle's electromagnetic charge to be $q/m$ of the electron charge. For the elementary case $q = 1$: charge $e/m$.
3. **Braiding.** Two quasiparticles winding around each other pick up a phase $2\pi/m$, by the CS chapter's linking-number formula.
4. **For $\nu = 1/3$.** Charge $e/3$, braiding phase $e^{2\pi i /3}$.
