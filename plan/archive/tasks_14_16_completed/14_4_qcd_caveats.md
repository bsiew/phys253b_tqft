## Content

1. **$F \wedge \tilde F$ is locally a total derivative** but not globally trivial. The topological charge is exact only on compact manifolds without boundary.
2. **Semiclassical reasoning.** Instanton-gas calculations are valid at weak coupling. At strong coupling (i.e., physical QCD), lattice methods are needed. State which results rely on semiclassics and which have been lattice-verified.
3. **$\chi_t$ is Euclidean and theory-side.** It is defined in Euclidean correlation functions. Its observable consequences (meson masses, axion mass) require analytic continuation and hadronization models. These intermediate steps are where model-dependence enters.
4. **nEDM measurement is clean.** The measurement itself is a precision null test. But extracting $\bar\theta$ from $d_n$ involves hadronic matrix elements (chiral perturbation theory, lattice QCD).
5. **Axion phenomenology: mass vs couplings.** The mass $m_a \propto \sqrt{\chi_t}/f_a$ is robust (model-independent). The detection couplings ($g_{a\gamma\gamma}$, $g_{aee}$, $g_{aNN}$) depend on the UV completion.
