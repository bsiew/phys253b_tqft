\chapter{Global Chern--Simons and level quantization for general \texorpdfstring{$G$}{G}}\label{app:cs-level}

\emph{Placeholder --- to be drafted in Stage VI (Task C.1).}

Extends the $SU(2)$ level-quantization argument of Chapter 5 to a general compact simple simply connected $G$ by fixing the invariant bilinear form so that $\tr((g^{-1}dg)^{\wedge 3})/24\pi^2$ generates $H^3(G,\mathbb{Z})$. Also covers spin-structure subtleties for $U(1)$ Chern--Simons on non-spin 3-manifolds and the framing anomaly.
