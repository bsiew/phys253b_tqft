## Goal

Produce one-page physical (paper) cheat cards to hold during the talk: not slides, not the script. Just enough to pick up after a brain-blank moment.

## Contents (per card)

1. **Board layout diagram** — small sketch of which equation lives where.
2. **Minute checkpoints** — "by minute 10 I should be at level quantization; by minute 18 at Wilson loops".
3. **3 hardest anticipated questions with prepared answers** — e.g.:
   - "Why is level quantization an integer and not half-integer?" → spin structure caveat, cite SeibergWitten2016Spin.
   - "How does this relate to the Jones polynomial exactly?" → state the Witten 1989 result, don't try to derive.
   - "Isn't fault-tolerance oversold given the current hardware?" → experimental status from Ch 13 §13.3.
4. **Safety phrases** for when stuck: "Let me come back to this", "That's a great question, I'd need to think carefully", "The short answer is X, see Chapter Y of our paper for the long answer".

## Acceptance criteria

- Two cheat cards printed, folded, in the presenter's pocket on the day.
- Font big enough to read at arm's length.
