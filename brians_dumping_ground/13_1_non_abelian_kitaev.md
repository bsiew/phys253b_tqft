## Goal

Introduce non-abelian anyons as the substrate for fault-tolerant quantum computation, following Kitaev's 2003 proposal.

## Content

1. **Topological degeneracy.** $N$ non-abelian anyons of a fixed fusion type have a degenerate ground space whose dimension grows exponentially in $N$.
2. **Topological protection.** Local perturbations commute with the ground-space projector to exponential accuracy in system size; the logical qubits are inaccessible to local noise.
3. **Braiding as unitary gate.** Exchanging two anyons effects a unitary transformation on the ground space; a computable sequence of braids implements a gate.
