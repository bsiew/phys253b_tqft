## Goal

Summarize the Nakamura–Liang–Gardner–Manfra 2020 experiment that directly observed anyonic braiding at the $\nu = 1/3$ Laughlin state. Identify precisely which theoretical prediction (the CS-Wilson-loop braiding phase $e^{2\pi i/3}$) was tested.

## Content

1. **Device.** GaAs/AlGaAs heterostructure, Fabry–Pérot-like interferometer with two quantum point contacts defining the interference paths.
2. **Observable.** Interference pattern oscillates as magnetic flux through the enclosed area is changed. At $\nu = 1/3$, a discrete jump in phase is observed whenever a bulk quasiparticle enters the interference region — the **braiding phase**.
3. **Measured value.** $\Delta\phi \approx 2\pi/3$, consistent with the Laughlin quasiparticle braiding.
4. **Theoretical prediction cited.** From the CS chapter Example `ex:abelian-wilson` and Chapter 11 §11.3: $e^{2\pi i / m} = e^{2\pi i/3}$ for $\nu = 1/3$.
