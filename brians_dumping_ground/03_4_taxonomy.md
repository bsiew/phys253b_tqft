## Goal

One short paragraph each on the three categories of TQFT we'll encounter.

## Content

1. **Schwarz-type TQFTs** (e.g., Chern–Simons, BF): defined by a metric-free action. Classical equations of motion are nontrivial (flat connections, etc.).
2. **Witten-type / cohomological TQFTs** (topological twists of supersymmetric theories): arise by twisting a supersymmetric theory; observables are cohomology classes of a BRST-like operator.
3. **Invertible TQFTs**: those for which $Z(M)$ is an invertible complex number and $Z(\Sigma)$ is one-dimensional. Examples include theta-terms, anomaly-inflow bulks. Appear in Chapter 8.
