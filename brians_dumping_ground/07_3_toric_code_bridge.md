## Goal

Show explicitly that the untwisted 3D Dijkgraaf–Witten theory with $G = \mathbb{Z}_2$ is the long-wavelength limit of the toric code. Cross-reference Chapter 6 (BF = DW) and Chapter 9 (toric code).

## Content

1. Recall from Chapter 6: compact $U(1)$ BF at level 2 = $\mathbb{Z}_2$ gauge theory = untwisted DW with $G = \mathbb{Z}_2$.
2. Toric code Hamiltonian $H = -\sum_v A_v - \sum_p B_p$ where $A_v, B_p$ are the star and plaquette stabilizers.
3. Ground space: $\prod A_v = \prod B_p = 1$, giving a $\mathbb{Z}_2$ gauge theory on the lattice.
4. Infrared limit recovers the continuum $\mathbb{Z}_2$ DW partition function.
