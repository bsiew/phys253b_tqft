## Acceptance criteria

- Both authors' board scripts fit in 25 minutes measured with a timer.
- Board-layout plan identifies which equations stay up for the full 50 minutes (CS action top-left, level quantization top-right, recurring diagrams bottom-right).
- Joint dry-run confirms the handoff at minute 25 is smooth (Brian's GSD discussion -> Helena's Hall-conductance pickup).
- Each author has printed a 1-page physical cheat card (not slides).
- Final PDF is 110-120 pages main + roughly 10-15 pages appendix. If outside, cut before submit.
- Submission uploaded successfully with confirmation.

## Dependencies

- Depends on: Stage VI complete (paper ready).
- Nothing blocks this; this is the last stage.

## Risks

- **Running over time live.** Blackboard talks routinely run 30% long on first delivery. Task 15.3 and 15.4 are explicit guardrails, and Task 15.4 must happen before the final day.
- **Not practicing the minute-10 level-quantization showpiece enough.** Brian should practice this out loud at least 5 times; it looks simple but requires clean board presence.
- **Q&A ambush.** Mitigation: Task 15.5 includes a "three hardest questions we expect, with prepared answers" list.
- **Submission policy surprise.** Some courses want individual LaTeX sources plus PDFs plus bib. Confirm the format on Day 9 morning, not Day 9 evening.
