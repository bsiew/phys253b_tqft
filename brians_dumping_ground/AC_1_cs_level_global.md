## Goal

Collect the technical details of global Chern–Simons that the main CS chapter references but does not fully carry: the level-quantization calculation extended beyond $SU(2)$, the spin-structure subtleties for abelian CS, and the framing anomaly.

## Content

1. **Level quantization for general compact simple simply-connected $G$.** Sketch the argument that $\tr((g^{-1}dg)^{\wedge 3})/(24\pi^2)$ represents the generator of $H^3(G,\mathbb{Z})$ after appropriate normalization of $\tr$. The main CS chapter proves $SU(2)$; this appendix states the general case and cites.
2. **Abelian $U(1)_k$ on non-spin manifolds.** Discuss the $k \in \mathbb{Z}$ vs. $k \in 2\mathbb{Z}$ vs. $k \in \mathbb{Z} + 1/2$ cases depending on spin structure. Cite Seiberg–Witten 2016 for the careful treatment.
3. **Framing anomaly.** The Wilson-loop expectation value depends on a framing; the dependence is $e^{2\pi i h_R \cdot n}$ where $h_R$ is the conformal weight and $n$ the relative framing. Cite Witten 1989.
4. **Shifted level $k \to k + h^\vee$.** One-loop quantum effect mentioned in the CS chapter; elaborated here.
