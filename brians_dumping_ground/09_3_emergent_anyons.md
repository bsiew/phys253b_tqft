## Goal

Identify the 4 anyon types of the toric code ($1, e, m, \epsilon$), compute their braiding statistics explicitly, and identify them as charged 0-form and 1-form symmetry-defect endpoints from the Chapter 6/8 perspective.

## Content

1. **Anyon types.**
   - $1$: vacuum (no excitation).
   - $e$ (electric charge): endpoint of an open $Z$-string, i.e., local violation of $A_v$.
   - $m$ (magnetic flux): endpoint of an open $X$-string, i.e., local violation of $B_p$.
   - $\epsilon = e \times m$ (fermion): both.
2. **Statistics.**
   - $e \times e = 1$, $m \times m = 1$, $e \times m = \epsilon$ (fusion rules).
   - $e$ and $m$ are bosons (self-braiding trivial).
   - $\epsilon$ is a fermion.
   - Mutual braiding: winding $e$ around $m$ produces a $-1$ phase. Verify explicitly using the string operator algebra.
3. **Bridge to Part I.**
   - $e$-string = Wilson line for the $\mathbb{Z}_2$ gauge field in the BF/DW description.
   - $m$-string = 't Hooft surface.
   - $e$–$m$ mutual braiding = the BF linking phase from Chapter 6.

## Acceptance criteria
