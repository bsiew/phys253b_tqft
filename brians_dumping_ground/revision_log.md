## Tagging convention adopted in this revision

Every task file now has a `Priority:` field:

- **`core`** — must-have for the paper to earn the grade. If this task slips, the paper is in trouble.
- **`important`** — significantly strengthens the paper (e.g., the Witten–Jones bridge). First-cut candidates if the schedule slips.
- **`stretch`** — nice-to-have. Cut first without regret if time-constrained.

Tags are advisory, not binding. The trim ladder in `plan_of_attack.tex` Section 7 governs the actual cut order.
