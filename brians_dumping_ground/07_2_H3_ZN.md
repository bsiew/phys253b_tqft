# Task 7.2 — Chapter 7 §7.2 Compute $H^3(\mathbb{Z}_N, U(1))$ via the Bar Resolution

- **Status:** done
- **Owner:** Brian
- **Duration:** 2 hours
- **Stage:** [III](../stages/stage_III_partI_B.md)

## Goal

Establish $H^3(\mathbb{Z}_N, U(1)) = \mathbb{Z}_N$ cleanly. The main text should present the classification and a concrete representative cocycle; the full bar-resolution derivation is Appendix D material unless the schedule is clearly ahead.

## Derivation

1. **Bar resolution**. The bar resolution $B_\bullet(G)$ of $\mathbb{Z}$ as a trivial $G$-module has $B_n = \mathbb{Z}[G^{n+1}]$ with boundary
   $\partial(g_0, g_1, \ldots, g_n) = \sum_{i=0}^n (-1)^i (g_0, \ldots, \hat{g}_i, \ldots, g_n)$.
2. **Group cochains**. $C^n(G, U(1)) = \mathrm{Hom}_G(B_n, U(1))$, isomorphic to maps $G^n \to U(1)$.
3. **Coboundary**. For $f: G^n \to U(1)$:
   $(\delta f)(g_1, \ldots, g_{n+1}) = f(g_2, \ldots, g_{n+1}) \prod_{i=1}^n f(g_1, \ldots, g_i g_{i+1}, \ldots, g_{n+1})^{(-1)^i} \cdot f(g_1, \ldots, g_n)^{(-1)^{n+1}}$.
4. **For $G = \mathbb{Z}_N$, $n = 3$**. Compute the 3-cocycle condition explicitly: a map $\omega: \mathbb{Z}_N^3 \to U(1)$ satisfying
   $\omega(b,c,d)\omega(a, b+c, d)\omega(a,b,c) = \omega(a+b, c, d)\omega(a, b, c+d)$.
5. **Canonical representative**. The cocycle $\omega_p(a,b,c) = \exp(2\pi i p \lfloor (b+c)/N \rfloor a / N)$ for $p = 0, 1, \ldots, N-1$ represents the $N$ distinct classes.
6. **Modulo coboundaries**. Show cocycles differing by a coboundary $\delta \beta$ for $\beta: \mathbb{Z}_N^2 \to U(1)$ are equivalent. The space of equivalence classes is $\mathbb{Z}_N$.

## Sanity checks

- $H^3(\mathbb{Z}_2, U(1)) = \mathbb{Z}_2$: check against known result (the "trivial" and "semion" twists).
- $H^3(\mathbb{Z}_N, U(1)) \cong \mathbb{Z}_N$ matches, e.g., Dijkgraaf–Witten 1990.

## Acceptance criteria

- Cocycle $\omega_p$ written down explicitly.
- Verification of the cocycle condition for $\omega_p$ in full.
- Explanation of why $\omega_p$ and $\omega_q$ are inequivalent for $p \neq q \mod N$.
- Main-text version fits in about 1.5-2 pages; overflow goes to Appendix D.

## Risks

- **Sign conventions in the bar resolution.** Multiple conventions exist; pick one and stick with it.
- **Getting the floor function right.** The $\lfloor (b+c)/N \rfloor$ arises from the mod-$N$ addition in $\mathbb{Z}_N$; verify carefully.
