## Goal

State the SymTFT (Symmetry Topological Field Theory) slogan, include one picture of the three-layer "sandwich" construction, and cite for details. Hard cap: **one page**.

## Content

1. **Slogan.** The symmetry data of a $d$-dimensional QFT $T$ can be packaged as a $(d+1)$-dimensional topological field theory $Z_{\text{sym}}$ sandwiched between two boundaries: a physical boundary realizing $T$ and a "symmetry boundary" encoding the global-symmetry data.
2. **Picture.** Three-layer sandwich: bulk SymTFT, physical boundary on one side, symmetry boundary on the other. Gauge transformations, anomalies, non-invertible symmetries all live in the bulk.
3. **Example.** For 2D rational CFT with a finite group $G$ global symmetry, the SymTFT is untwisted 3D Dijkgraaf–Witten theory at $G$ (or its twisted version for anomalous $G$).
4. **Why we stop here.** Fuller SymTFT development requires modular tensor category technology; we cite Freed–Moore–Teleman 2024 and Bhardwaj et al 2023 lectures.
